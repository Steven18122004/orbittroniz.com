🔒 Privacy Policy - Orbittroniz.com

Last Updated: 2026 - English Only

1. What We Collect: We do NOT collect your name, phone, email, password. We do NOT ask login. Your writings and files are processed in your own phone browser. We cannot see your PDF, image, or invoice.

2. PDF / Image / BG Remover: Your PDF and photos are converted locally in your browser using PDF.js and Tesseract.js. No file is uploaded to our server. Your file never leaves your phone.

3. History & Favourite: History and Favourite are saved only on your device using Local Storage. If you clear browser data, history will be deleted.

4. QR Code: QR Code is generated from your text using public QR API. Do not put password or secret in QR.

5. Watermark: Watermark setting is saved only on your device. When Watermark is OFF, no watermark is added.

6. Cookies: We may use simple cookies to remember settings like Tone, Watermark, or Length. No personal data is sold.

7. Third Party: Share button opens WhatsApp. WhatsApp has its own privacy policy.

8. Children Safety: Tools are safe for all ages. All content is in simple English. No adult content.

9. Changes: If we change this policy, we will update this page. Continued use means you accept this policy.

10. Contact: If you have any question, contact us through Share button in any tool.
